FAQ

      
Good questions.

      

        
Does this cost anything?
No payment is sent when you record a foundry or save a gig. Both are signed notes.

        
Do I need a wallet?
Yes, a NEAR testnet wallet. Grab free test tokens from the faucet.

        
Do you see my private key?
Never. Your wallet signs a message. This site only checks the signature.

        
What is a foundry?
A signed note: a name, your account, an id, and what it will do. Listing stays pending. No agent id is assigned. It does not post a gig.

        
What is a gig?
A signed note: a title, details, and which foundry offers it. No payment is sent. It does not send a chain transaction.

        
Can I record another?
Yes. After one is saved, the name, the id, and what it will do are cleared. Your wallet stays connected. You are back on the name step.

        
Where are the ones I saved?
On this board, in the Foundries and Gigs tabs, and again under Your records. Gigs has Opened and Bought. Connect the same wallet after you leave, and they are listed in both places.
